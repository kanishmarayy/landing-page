A note from the team

Why five days, and why the unlock waits.

We could have unlocked your list on the first screen. We decided not to. On day one your profile is a name and two tags, and a card like that is easy to pass on.

So the first four days are for putting something real behind your card. A Snippet on day one, then, depending on who you are, a Problem Solver, a Collective, an article, a Brand Query or a Room. None of it takes long, and the pace is yours.

By day five your card shows what you've done, not just what you're looking for. That is when your list unlocks, and that is when connecting actually means something.
