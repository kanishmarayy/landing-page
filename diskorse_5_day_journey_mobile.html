How it works

From signing up to your first potential customers in five days.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you seek and offer

    Sign in, pick the categories you care about, and add a few tags for what you can offer and what you’re looking for. Then pick who you are, so your five days fit you.

  2. 2

    Do one small task a day

    Each day opens one feature, like posting a Snippet or starting a Problem Solver. The next day opens once you finish the one before, so you can go at your own pace.

  3. 3

    Unlock your potential customers

    On day five, you see the people who need what you offer. Swipe on the ones you like, and reach out to your matches through what they already have on Diskorse.

  4. 4

    Get a new list every week

    After your five free days, a weekly plan brings you a new list of potential customers and 10 Brand Query replies.

Why five days

Why five days?

Diskorse gives you different ways to talk to people: Snippets, Problem Solver, Collective, Brand Queries, Counsel and Articles. Over five days you try them one at a time, with one small task a day picked for who you are. Every task builds your brand presence, and it shows us who your ideal customer is, so we can introduce you to the right people.

Pick who you are to see your five days.

Day five

Your potential customers, unlocked.

On day five, Diskorse helps you discover the people who need exactly what you offer, and offer what you need. Swipe on the most promising ones and if they like what you have so far, it’s a match!

Matched on both sides
They offer what you’re seeking, or they’re seeking what you offer.
Private until it’s mutual
You’ll see how many people swiped on you, but not who. They’re somewhere in your list, so swipe on the ones you like to find out.
Reach out with something real
Every match shows what they already have on Diskorse. You can book their Counsel session, read their article, comment on their Snippet or reply to their Brand Query. You can still send a message.
Customers for your Problem Solver
For Startup Owners, your list includes the people most likely to use what you posted in Problem Solver, so you can test the idea with real customers before you build it.
